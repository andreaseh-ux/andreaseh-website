I’ve been traveling in my campervan for five years, chasing the most beautiful places and unique experiences. What started as a hobby has grown into a passion for capturing the world through a different perspective.

Today I create cinematic content with FPV drone, DJI Mavic 4 Pro and Sony α7 IV camera for destinations, hotels, cabins, glamping, sports, real estate, events and brands — with a focus on nature, action and real experiences.
