04 — ABOUT

Curious by nature.
Always looking for the next view.

I'm Andreas Holst, a Norwegian visual creator and drone pilot with a passion for travel, adventure and cinematic storytelling.

For the past five years, I've been creating visual content around Norway and beyond — working with destinations, stays, brands and experiences that are worth discovering.

My toolkit combines FPV drone, traditional drone and Sony α7 IV. The goal is simple: create images and films that feel authentic, cinematic and make people stop scrolling.
